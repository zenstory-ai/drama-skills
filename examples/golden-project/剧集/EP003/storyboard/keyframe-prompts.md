# Keyframe prompts

## KEY-EP003-01-START

Vertical 9:16 medium wide shot at the east entrance of Anhe Noodle Shop on a stormy night. Chen Yuan in his dark-blue work apron stands by the doorway pulling the metal rolling shutter halfway down by its cord, heavy rain splashing in cold blue light across the stone threshold outside.

## KEY-EP003-01-END

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot inside the east entrance on a stormy night. Beneath the half-lowered metal rolling shutter, Zhou Haiping—a square-faced man around fifty with greying temples in a rain-drenched brown jacket—ducks into the shop clutching a rusted rectangular mooncake tin box against his chest with both hands, while Chen Yuan pauses holding the shutter cord.

## KEY-EP003-02-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot near the east entrance on a rainy night. Zhou Haiping in his soaked brown jacket stands dripping water on the tile floor while clutching the closed rusted mooncake tin box to his chest, catching his breath as he looks at Chen Yuan, whose right hand still holds the shutter cord.

## KEY-EP003-02-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up two-shot near the east entrance on a rainy night. Chen Yuan's right hand has stopped still on the shutter pull-cord as he looks intently at Zhou Haiping; Zhou Haiping stands holding the rusted mooncake tin box against his wet brown jacket under warm interior light and cold blue rain light.

## KEY-EP003-03-START

Vertical 9:16 medium shot across the worn north wooden counter on a rainy night. Zhou Haiping in his wet brown jacket steps up to the counter holding the closed rusted rectangular mooncake tin box in both hands just above the wood, while Chen Yuan watches from behind the counter.

## KEY-EP003-03-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter on a rainy night. The closed rusted mooncake tin box sits in the center of the wooden counter surrounded by a clear wet ring of rainwater; Zhou Haiping's callused right hand stays pressed flat on the closed tin lid while Chen Yuan looks down from across the counter, no readable brand text.

## KEY-EP003-04-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot at the north wooden counter on a rainy night. Chen Yuan in his dark-blue apron flips through the yellowed index pages of the cloth-bound old ledger beside the closed rusted tin box, while Zhou Haiping stands across the counter with his right hand still resting on the tin lid.

## KEY-EP003-04-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up across the north wooden counter on a rainy night. Chen Yuan holds the old ledger open to a yellowed entry page and lifts his gaze to look at Zhou Haiping; beside the ledger, Zhou Haiping has lifted his right hand off the rusted mooncake tin box, leaving the box closed on the counter, clean reserved entry area on the page for postproduction lettering.

## KEY-EP003-05-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot at the north wooden counter on a rainy night. The closed rusted mooncake tin box and the open old ledger sit side by side on the wooden counter between Zhou Haiping in his wet brown jacket and Chen Yuan in his dark-blue work apron.

## KEY-EP003-05-END

Vertical 9:16 medium shot looking past the north wooden counter toward the back-kitchen stove on a rainy night. In the foreground the closed rusted mooncake tin box and the open old ledger sit side by side on the counter; in the background Chen Yuan turns to ignite the blue flame of the noodle stove while Zhou Haiping waits outside the counter.
