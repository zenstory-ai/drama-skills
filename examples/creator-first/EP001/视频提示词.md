# EP001 视频提示词

## MOTION-EP001-001 · 攥紧的茶杯

- 分镜：SHOT-EP001-001
- 时长：3s
- 生成方式：文生视频
- 输入参考图：无（创作者已明确选择文生视频）。
- 静态视觉锚点：A heavy middle-aged hand with a pine-green service-jacket cuff grips a double-walled glass tea tumbler on a glass desktop in a vertical extreme close-up, cold green tea inside with the leaves sunk to the bottom, the tumbler on a dark round coaster with its steel lid set aside, and a cold bluish screen glow falling from frame left over a soft, out-of-focus office.
- 起始帧：右手攥着茶杯杯身，冷茶面微颤。
- 状态链：手攥着茶杯 → 哄笑一起，手再攥紧，茶面抖出细纹 → 指节发白
- 终点：指节攥到发白，茶面还在抖。

### 可复制提示词
> A heavy middle-aged hand with a pine-green service-jacket cuff grips a double-walled glass tea tumbler on a glass desktop in a vertical extreme close-up, cold green tea inside with the leaves sunk to the bottom, the tumbler on a dark round coaster with its steel lid set aside, and a cold bluish screen glow falling from frame left over a soft, out-of-focus office. From a laptop speaker just off the left edge, a man's voice says in Chinese, loud and amused, “火箭军？四个号，加起来四个粉吧。”, and a burst of laughter follows through the same small speaker. As the laughter starts, the knuckles tighten further and turn pale, and the cold tea trembles in fine rings. The camera does not move. End with the knuckles white around the glass and the tea still trembling. No subtitles, captions or dialogue text overlays at any moment; no other text, no logo, no second tumbler.
