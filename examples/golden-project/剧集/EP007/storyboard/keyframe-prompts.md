# Keyframe prompts

## KEY-EP007-01-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot at the north wooden counter of Anhe Noodle Shop before dawn. Under warm counter lamp light with dim pre-dawn cool light at the shutter seam, Chen Yuan in his dark-blue apron sets two kraft paper envelopes side by side on the wood beside the open rusted tin box of cash while Zhou Haiping watches across the counter.

## KEY-EP007-01-END

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot across the north wooden counter before dawn. Two kraft envelopes lie side by side next to the open tin box of bundled cash; Chen Yuan rests his right hand on the blank envelope as he speaks firmly to Zhou Haiping, no readable currency text.

## KEY-EP007-02-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot across the north wooden counter before dawn. Zhou Haiping in his brown jacket places both hands on the sides of the open rusted tin box holding the kraft-banded cash, preparing to slide it across the wooden countertop toward Chen Yuan.

## KEY-EP007-02-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter before dawn. Zhou Haiping has pushed the open rusted tin box of bundled cash across the counter to Chen Yuan's side next to the blank kraft envelope, his hands resting calmly on the wood as both men share a relieved look.

## KEY-EP007-03-START

Vertical 9:16 medium wide shot inside Anhe Noodle Shop before dawn. At the east metal rolling shutter Liang Feng in his dark courier jacket ducks into the shop with a grin while reaching into his jacket pocket, as Chen Yuan and Zhou Haiping turn toward him from the north wooden counter.

## KEY-EP007-03-END

Vertical 9:16 medium three-shot at the north wooden counter before dawn. Liang Feng stands beside Zhou Haiping and places his heavily creased ten-yuan note right on top of the kraft-banded cash stack inside the open tin box, while Chen Yuan looks down from behind the counter, no readable currency text.

## KEY-EP007-04-START

Vertical 9:16 medium three-shot across the north wooden counter before dawn. Chen Yuan behind the counter looks quizzically at Liang Feng while holding a pen beside the blank kraft envelope; Liang Feng and Zhou Haiping stand side by side across the counter beside the open tin box.

## KEY-EP007-04-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up three-shot at the north wooden counter before dawn. Chen Yuan, Zhou Haiping, and Liang Feng lean in together over the counter as Chen Yuan writes on the front of the kraft envelope while the other two steady its edges, clean reserved area on the envelope for postproduction lettering.

## KEY-EP007-05-START

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter before dawn. Chen Yuan in his dark-blue apron lifts the kraft-banded cash stack together with the creased ten-yuan note out of the rusted tin box and slides them into the kraft relay meal-voucher envelope on the counter, no readable currency text.

## KEY-EP007-05-END

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot across the north wooden counter before dawn. With the sealed kraft meal-voucher envelope resting under his left hand and the rusted tin box now empty, Chen Yuan uses his right hand to pass the yellowed IOU sheet back into Zhou Haiping's receiving hands.

## KEY-EP007-06-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot at the north wooden counter before dawn. Chen Yuan writes the updated status on the yellowed page of the open old ledger with his right hand, while across the counter Zhou Haiping holds the returned yellowed IOU sheet carefully in both hands without tearing it.

## KEY-EP007-06-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter before dawn. Alongside the open old ledger and the sealed meal-voucher envelope, Zhou Haiping's both hands carefully fold the yellowed IOU back along its original four creases and place it inside the now-empty rusted mooncake tin box, clean reserved ledger area for postproduction lettering.
