# Keyframe prompts

## KEY-EP004-01-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot at the north wooden counter at night. Beside an empty ceramic noodle bowl, Zhou Haiping in his drying brown jacket wipes his hands on a cloth towel before reaching for the closed rusted mooncake tin box on the counter, while Chen Yuan watches from behind the counter.

## KEY-EP004-01-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter at night. The rusted mooncake tin box lies open on the wood revealing a neat kraft-banded stack of cash inside; beside it Chen Yuan holds open a four-times-folded sheet of yellowed lined paper facing upward, clean reserved area on the paper for postproduction lettering.

## KEY-EP004-02-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot across the north wooden counter at night. Chen Yuan in his dark-blue apron holds the unfolded yellowed lined IOU sheet open above the counter, looking down at its front side, while Zhou Haiping stands across the counter beside the open tin box of cash.

## KEY-EP004-02-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up across the north wooden counter at night. Zhou Haiping with greying temples speaks earnestly across the open tin box of cash, while Chen Yuan raises his eyes from the unfolded yellowed IOU sheet in his hands to look at him, clean reserved area on the paper for postproduction lettering.

## KEY-EP004-03-START

Vertical 9:16 close-up at the north wooden counter at night. Chen Yuan's hands hold the unfolded yellowed lined IOU sheet under the warm counter lamp, where faint ink strokes bleeding through from the back of the paper catch his eye, the open tin box of cash resting below.

## KEY-EP004-03-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter at night. Chen Yuan has turned the unfolded yellowed IOU sheet over to its reverse side in both hands and shifts his gaze from the back of the paper to the kraft-banded stack of cash inside the open rusted tin box on the counter, clean reserved area on the paper for postproduction lettering.

## KEY-EP004-04-START

Vertical 9:16 medium two-shot across the north wooden counter at night. Chen Yuan stands behind the counter holding the reverse-side-up yellowed IOU sheet while looking questioningly at Zhou Haiping; the open rusted tin box with its bundled cash sits between them on the counter.

## KEY-EP004-04-END

Vertical 9:16 medium close-up at the north wooden counter at night. Chen Yuan has placed the unfolded yellowed IOU flat on the counter beside the old ledger and withdraws his hands without touching the kraft-banded cash inside the open rusted tin box, while Zhou Haiping stands across the counter.
